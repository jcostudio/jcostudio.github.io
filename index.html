About me

I'm JC, a social media strategist, content creator, and curator with a Bachelor of Arts in Communications from Cal State Fullerton. I shape how brands look and feel online, from content strategy and photography to logos and full campaigns.

My foundation is service. My very first job was at Disneyland, and that training taught me how the smallest details create the magic of an experience. I've carried that Disney touch with me ever since. I went on to spend more than fifteen years in fine dining, where white-glove service only works when it feels seamless, and I delivered Teslas in the Model S era, when it was a six-figure luxury car. Working with high-end, luxury names, paired with an artist's eye, taught me what brands are looking for. That standard is part of how I work.

Now I'm bringing that eye to what I studied. Through Orange Circle Media Agency, which I'm relaunching, I've created for MAMM Alliance, ZzyXx Shoes, Fitness Grill Kitchen, Versai Wine Bar, and Big Chopsticks, and I took on campaign management, social media, and sponsorships for the 2015 Newport Beach Film Festival's French Spotlight.

I'm drawn to calm, beautiful spaces and I bring that care into everything I make. I'd love to create for yours.

The strategy: let the art do the talking

MAMM Alliance raises scholarship funds for fine and performing arts students, but its social presence was flat: logos, show titles, and headshots, with a team still learning the platforms. I audited the accounts, defined a brand voice rooted in the arts, and built a content strategy and editorial calendar around one idea: every post should feel like a glimpse of opening night.

I curated rich, high-contrast imagery of concert halls, orchestras, choirs, and dancers, then paired it with quote graphics, so the feed worked as an exhibition that made people curious about the mission.

Teaching the team to lead

A strategy only lasts if the team can run it. I trained the board and volunteers to use social media like professionals: hashtag strategy, SEO, scheduling, community engagement, and Google Analytics and platform insights to measure the return on their time. They learned how to plan fundraisers and events that build awareness of the mission.

Campaigns that fill the room

I designed the flyers and campaign graphics for MAMM's fundraisers and event nights, covered the organization at the Fullerton Founder's Day Parade, and turned those photos into branded tiles for social. I also redesigned MAMM's website, so the story stayed consistent from the feed to the front door.
